# Before the Dragons — Full VO Script + Shot List, Episodes 1–5

Timing model: 8 clips × 8.00 s = 64 s. Narration ~18–22 words per clip (deep, slow delivery ≈ 2.4 words/sec), landing ~0.5 s before the clip ends. Shot column is the visual brief that feeds the MiniMax prompt.

## EPISODE 2 — The Long Night

**Cold open card:** `BEFORE THE DRAGONS · PART 2`

| # | VO (verbatim) | Shot | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | For four thousand years, men and the children kept the peace. Then the sun failed to come back. | Grey dawn over a snowbound plain; the light visibly drains out of the sky in a slow time-lapse until the frame is near black. | `THE LONG NIGHT` |
| 2 | Winter arrived and refused to leave. A generation was born and died without ever seeing summer. | A frozen village half-buried in drifts; a woman drags firewood past frost-covered doorways as a child watches from a shuttered window. | — |
| 3 | Crops failed. Herds froze. And in the darkness, something walked south that was not alive. | Deep night in a snowfield. Blue-white light glints between trees; a tall pale silhouette with mirror-bright armour moves at the edge of visibility and is gone. | `THE OTHERS` |
| 4 | Their dead rose and marched with them. Every village they took, they added to their column. | Low tracking shot along a marching line of frost-rimed corpses with pale blue eyes, walking in silence through knee-deep snow. | — |
| 5 | One man went north to find help — the Last Hero, with twelve companions, a horse and a dog. | Blizzard. A lone armoured figure leads a horse up a ridge; behind them, twelve dark shapes are strung out and falling further behind with every step. | `THE LAST HERO` |
| 6 | He lost every one of them. What he found, and what he traded for it, no story agrees. | The same figure alone at the treeline, sword broken, kneeling before three small hooded figures who emerge from the snow to meet them. | — |
| 7 | The dead were broken in one last battle, and men decided they would never trust the north again. | Aerial over a huge ice wall under construction: thousands of tiny figures hauling ice blocks up ramps as the barrier stretches from horizon to horizon. | `THE WALL` |
| 8 | Seven hundred feet of ice, three hundred miles long, and a brotherhood sworn to hold it. Eight thousand quiet years followed. They would not last. | Slow crane up the black face of the finished Wall to a line of men in black cloaks standing on top, looking north into white nothing. | `PART 3 — THE EMPIRE OF CHAINS` |

## EPISODE 3 — The Empire of Chains

**Cold open card:** `BEFORE THE DRAGONS · PART 3`

| # | VO (verbatim) | Shot | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | While Westeros froze, the first true empire in the world was being built on the other side of the sea. | Aerial at golden hour over a vast harsh red-brown coastline with a colossal walled city of stepped brick pyramids on the shore. | `OLD GHIS` |
| 2 | Ghis did not invent cruelty. It industrialised it. | Long overhead shot of an enormous brick quarry where thousands of chained labourers move stone in ordered lines under overseers. | — |
| 3 | Its wealth was people. Bought, bred, counted, and worked until they were replaced. | Ground-level shot down a market row: iron collars being fitted, a clerk marking a wax tablet, a line of people waiting without expression. | `THE FIRST SLAVE EMPIRE` |
| 4 | Its power was the legion — lockstep, disciplined, and utterly without mercy. | Low angle on a marching legion in bronze scale and tall shields, spears rising and falling in perfect unison, dust boiling around their feet. | `THE GHISCARI LEGIONS` |
| 5 | For centuries nothing on the continent could stand against that formation. Nothing needed to be. | Battlefield wide shot: a shield wall grinding forward over broken bodies; opposing banners collapse and are trampled. | — |
| 6 | Then, far to the west, on a peninsula of volcanoes, some shepherds went looking for lost sheep. | Hard cut to a bleak volcanic slope at dawn; two ragged shepherds climb between black lava rocks, calling into the wind. | `THE FOURTEEN FLAMES` |
| 7 | They found caves. In the caves they found something sleeping that had never been tamed. | Cave interior lit only by a deep red glow; a huge scaled flank rises and falls with slow breathing as the shepherds' torchlight finds an enormous closed eye. | — |
| 8 | Ghis had legions. Valyria was about to have dragons. That fight was only going to end one way. | The eye opens. Cut wide: a shepherd stands frozen as vast wings unfold behind them, filling the frame. | `PART 4 — FOURTEEN FLAMES` |

## EPISODE 5 — Five Wars, One Ash Field

**Cold open card:** `BEFORE THE DRAGONS · PART 5`

| # | VO (verbatim) | Shot | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ghis and Valyria fought five wars. Ghis won none of them. | Split-horizon wide: a legion in perfect formation on a plain, and above the ridge behind them, three dragons rising. | `FIVE WARS` |
| 2 | The legions did everything correctly. That was the problem — they stood in ranks. | Ground-level in the shield wall as officers dress the line; men lock shields and brace, eyes on the sky rather than the front. | — |
| 3 | Ranks are hard to break with cavalry. They are very easy to burn. | A wall of dragonfire rolls the length of the formation; shields blacken and men scatter as the line disintegrates. | — |
| 4 | War after war, the empire shrank. Cities changed hands, then changed shape. | Aerial over a burned coastal city, its brick pyramids slumped and glassy, smoke drifting over an empty harbour. | — |
| 5 | In the fifth war Valyria stopped taking territory and started removing it. | Night raid: repeated fire passes over a walled city, each pass leaving a wider ring of flame until the walls glow. | `THE FIFTH WAR` |
| 6 | They burned Ghis to the ground, tore down what was left, and ploughed the ruin under. | Dawn over the ruin field: teams of chained labourers dragging ploughs through ash and broken brick where a city stood. | — |
| 7 | Then they sowed the fields with salt, so that nothing would grow there again. | Close on hands scattering coarse white salt into black ash; camera pulls back to reveal the scale of the dead ground. | `SOWN WITH SALT` |
| 8 | The oldest empire in the world ended in a single afternoon. Nothing now stood between Valyria and everything else. | Slow aerial pull-back from the salted plain to a horizon of circling dragons under a red sky. | `PART 6 — THE FREEHOLD` |
