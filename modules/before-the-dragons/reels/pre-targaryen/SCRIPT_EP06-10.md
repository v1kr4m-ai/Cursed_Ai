## EPISODE 7 — The Andal Tide

**Cold open card:** `BEFORE THE DRAGONS · PART 7`

| # | VO (verbatim) | Shot | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Six thousand years ago, Westeros was invaded again — and this time it stuck. | Aerial over a fleet of longships crossing a grey sea toward a green coastline at dawn. | `THE ANDAL INVASION` |
| 2 | The Andals came out of the east with iron, and iron beats bronze every single time. | Close combat: an iron blade shears through a bronze sword and the bronze buckles; the bearer falls back. | `IRON vs BRONZE` |

| 4 | Where they landed, the weirwoods came down and the old gods went quiet. | Axes at the base of a carved white tree; the face is hacked away before the trunk falls into a cleared grove. | — |
| 5 | Kingdom by kingdom, the First Men lost the south. Marriage where it worked, slaughter where it did not. | Two shots in one move: a formal wedding in a hall, camera trucks right through a doorway into a burning yard of bodies. | — |

## EPISODE 8 — Ten Thousand Ships

**Cold open card:** `BEFORE THE DRAGONS · PART 8`

| # | VO (verbatim) | Shot | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Rhoynar lived along the greatest river in the world, and they had done it for a very long time. | Aerial along an immense slow river at golden hour, terraced water-cities and painted boats crowding both banks. | `THE RHOYNAR` |
| 2 | They were traders, not conquerors, and their water wizards kept their cities from ever being taken. | River-level shot: robed figures raise their hands and the river visibly swells against a stone wall, holding it. | — |
| 3 | Then Valyria wanted their trade routes, and being peaceful stopped being an option. | Wide on dragons crossing the river at low altitude while painted barges scatter beneath them. | — |
| 4 | Prince Garin raised a quarter of a million men. It was the largest army the Rhoyne had ever seen. | Enormous ground-level tracking shot down a river bank packed with assembled spearmen stretching past the horizon. | `GARIN THE GREAT` |
| 5 | Valyria burned it in an afternoon, then hung him in a golden cage above his own city. | A gilded cage suspended from a high stone arch above a smoking city; a figure inside watches the water below. | — |
| 6 | He cursed them from that cage. Within days the river rose and swallowed the city whole. | Flooding shot: dark water climbing marble steps, palaces sinking, mist closing over rooftops until nothing is left. | `GARIN'S CURSE` |
| 7 | One princess refused to die there. Nymeria loaded ten thousand ships and sailed west with the survivors. | Aerial over a colossal ragged fleet at dawn, decks crowded with people, sails patched, all pointed the same way. | `NYMERIA` |
| 8 | She burned the ships on a desert shore so no one could go back. Out of that came Dorne. | Night: the entire fleet burning on a dark shoreline, a woman standing on the sand watching, back to camera. | `PART 9 — THE DREAM OF FIRE` |

## EPISODE 10 — The Doom

**Cold open card:** `BEFORE THE DRAGONS · PART 10`

| # | VO (verbatim) | Shot | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
